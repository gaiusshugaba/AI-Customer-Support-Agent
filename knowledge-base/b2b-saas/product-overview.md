# Product Overview

## What is FlowStack?

FlowStack is a workflow automation platform that lets teams connect their business tools (CRM, support desk, billing, internal apps) without writing custom integration code. Customers use FlowStack to automate repetitive processes such as lead routing, ticket escalation, invoice reminders, and data syncing between systems.

## Plans

FlowStack is offered on three plans:

- **Starter** — up to 3 active workflows, 5,000 workflow executions/month, community support only.
- **Growth** — up to 25 active workflows, 50,000 executions/month, email support with 24-hour response SLA.
- **Enterprise** — unlimited workflows, custom execution volume, dedicated support with 4-hour response SLA, single sign-on (SSO), and a dedicated account manager.

## Core Concepts

- **Workflow**: A sequence of connected steps (trigger + actions) that runs automatically when a defined event occurs.
- **Trigger**: The event that starts a workflow (e.g., "new form submission," "invoice overdue").
- **Connector**: A pre-built integration to a third-party service (e.g., Salesforce, Zendesk, Stripe).
- **Execution**: A single run of a workflow from trigger to completion. Executions are what count against your monthly plan limit.

## Getting Started

New accounts start with a 14-day free trial on the Growth plan. No credit card is required to start a trial. At the end of the trial, accounts are automatically downgraded to Starter unless a paid plan is selected.
