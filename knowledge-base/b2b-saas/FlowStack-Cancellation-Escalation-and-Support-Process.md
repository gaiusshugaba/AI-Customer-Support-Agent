## How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. You'll be asked for a brief reason (optional). Cancellation stops future renewals. Your access continues until the end of the current paid period. Cancellation does not refund the current period.

## What happens to my data after cancellation?
Data is retained for 90 days after cancellation. During that period, you can reactivate the subscription and restore full access. After 90 days, data is permanently deleted according to the retention policy. You can export your data at any time before deletion from Settings → Workspace → Export.

## Can I pause my subscription instead of cancelling?
Pausing is available on Growth, Scale, and Enterprise plans. A paused workspace retains all data but cannot run new executions. Pause can be scheduled for up to 12 months. Billing resumes automatically when the pause ends or when you manually resume.

## Can I get a refund when I cancel?
Monthly plans are not refunded for the current period. Annual plans can be refunded pro-rata if cancelled within the first 14 days. Duplicate charges and confirmed billing errors are always refunded in full, regardless of cancellation.

## When should a support case be escalated?
Escalate when:
- The request involves security or unauthorized access.
- The customer disputes a specific charge and trusted information is insufficient.
- Required account information is unavailable.
- The approved knowledge base doesn't contain enough information to answer safely.
- The issue requires human review or a policy decision.
- The customer provides evidence that materially changes the case and automation cannot safely resolve it.
- The customer explicitly asks for a human.

## What does an active case mean?
An active case is an open escalation that's waiting for either customer information or human review. A customer having an active case does not mean every later message belongs to that case. The message must be relevant to the active case before it's treated as a follow-up. Unrelated messages route through normal support.

## What information should be collected for escalation?
When more information is needed, request only what is genuinely useful:
- Billing email
- Relevant date (of the transaction, incident, or error)
- Transaction amount and currency
- Invoice or receipt number
- Workflow name
- Visible error text or status code
- Redacted screenshot (secrets hidden)

Do not request passwords, full card numbers, CVVs, OTP codes, API secrets, or other authentication values.

## How should the assistant handle an escalation?
1. Acknowledge the customer's actual problem specifically.
2. Explain what information is still needed, if any.
3. State the next step clearly.
4. Avoid generic promises.
5. Avoid claiming a human has reviewed the case unless it's true.

## What is "live chat" handoff vs "ticket" handoff?
Live chat handoff connects the customer with a human agent in the same conversation window. Ticket handoff logs the case for asynchronous review by a support agent who will respond by email. The appropriate mode depends on urgency: security incidents and account lockouts usually need live chat; billing disputes and feature questions can go through tickets.

## How long does escalation take?
Ticket escalations receive a first response within 4 business hours for Growth plans, 2 business hours for Scale, and 1 business hour for Enterprise. Live chat handoffs are connected within 15 minutes during business hours (9am–6pm in the workspace's primary timezone).

## Can I close an escalation myself?
Yes, if the escalation is still in the "awaiting customer info" state. Reply to the case with "Please close this case" and the case status updates to "closed". Cases that are already with a human agent can only be closed by the agent.

## How do I reopen a closed case?
Reply to the case thread within 30 days of closure. The case reopens with the same case ID. After 30 days, a new case must be created — reply on the old thread will start a fresh case.

## What if I disagree with the outcome of an escalation?
Reply to the case and request a second review. It will be routed to a senior support agent. If the issue involves a policy decision you disagree with, ask for it to be escalated to a support manager.
